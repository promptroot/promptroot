A CLI or extension on another device wants to act on your behalf. Confirm the code below matches the one shown on that device, then click Authorize.

Format: 4 letters/digits, a hyphen, then 4 more (e.g. WDJB-MJHT).

What is this?

PromptRoot uses a device flow to give CLIs and editor extensions access to your account without asking for a password or generating a separate token. The CLI shows you a code; you confirm here while signed in.

You can revoke any active device session anytime from your profile.
