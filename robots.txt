# --- What is "Copen"? ---
# "Copen" stands for "Copy and Open".
# It is a feature that allows users to copy a prompt from our library and immediately
# open a target AI service in a new tab, ready for pasting.
# This creates a seamless workflow from our prompt library to your AI tools.
